## 4. Relationship Between the Assignments

Assignment 1 should establish the academic foundation for Assignment 2. Its thematic synthesis, critical appraisal, identified limitations, and future-research direction should help justify the proposed project.

The expected progression is:

1. Review and synthesize the current literature in Assignment 1.
2. Identify a clear and defensible research gap.
3. Convert that gap into a problem statement and research questions.
4. Define a feasible aim, objectives, scope, and methodology for Assignment 2.
5. Defend the proposal through slides, an oral presentation, and questions and answers.

## 5. Working Conventions

### 5.1. General Conventions

- Keep Assignment 1 materials inside `asg-1/`.
- Keep Assignment 2 materials inside `asg-2/`.
- Treat the **Overview** and **Project Details** sections in this file as the shared source of truth for project metadata, assignment mapping, and canonical terminology.
- Always use the canonical component names **Project System**, **Project Proposal**, **Presentation Slides**, and **Presentation (Oral Defence and Q&A)** consistently. Do not rename, merge, split, or substitute these terms.
- Treat `asg-2/02-project-core-idea.md` as the source of truth for the project's starting point, problem, and direction. Keep the Project Proposal, Project System, Presentation Slides, and Presentation (Oral Defence and Q&A) aligned with it.
- Do not use bold formatting within Project Proposal content. Use numbered headings and plain text for structure.
- Treat `asg-1/00-overview.md` and `asg-2/00-overview.md` as the working overviews for their respective assignments.
- Prefix overview and navigation documents with `00-`.
- Use an unnumbered page title, place the blockquote-style linked contents list immediately below it, and begin the numbered content with `1. Overview`.
- Clearly distinguish official requirements from recommendations and working assumptions.
- Do not alter files in `../quesiton/`; they are reference materials.
- Preserve the student's academic voice and comply with academic-integrity requirements.

### 5.2. Assignment 1 Research Conventions

- Treat `asg-1/00-overview.md` as the Assignment 1 working overview.
- Use peer-reviewed journal articles, conference papers, and suitable academic books.
- Do not invent references, findings, quotations, DOIs, page numbers, or publication details.
- Use APA referencing consistently and verify every citation against its source.
- Prefer literature from the last five years, including current-year work where available.
- Organize the review by themes and debates, not as isolated article summaries.
- Paraphrase carefully and critically compare methods, results, limitations, and gaps.
